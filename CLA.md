## Why this exists, in plain language

QA-AI-STLC is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Under that license, everyone who
contributes code keeps the copyright to their own contribution — the project as a whole can only
ever be licensed under terms every contributor agreed to. That is normal and does not need to
change for the project to stay a genuine, fully open-source project.

This agreement adds exactly **one** thing on top of what Apache-2.0 and the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) already require: it lets the
project maintainer(s) offer the _same contribution_ under different license terms **in addition
to** Apache-2.0 in the future (for example, a dual-licensed commercial edition), without having to
track down and re-ask every past contributor individually. It does **not** transfer your
copyright, does **not** stop you from using your own contribution however you like, and does
**not** change the license the project itself is available under today.

This agreement is required **in addition to**, not instead of, the DCO sign-off
(`git commit -s`) every commit already carries.
